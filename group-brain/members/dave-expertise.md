## Proven Frameworks

### 1. Warm Outbound Buyer Psychology

**Statement:** Personalized warm outbound sequences that mirror the buyer's own language convert 3x better than cold generic outreach.

**Evidence:**
- 5+ years of SaaS sales experience
- 47 warm sequences vs 120 cold sequences analyzed in 2025
- Warm metrics: 12% response rate, 8% win rate
- Cold metrics: 4% response rate, 1.2% win rate
- Pattern holds across 3 different products

**When It Works:**
- Buyer problem is well-defined (doesn't work for awareness campaigns)
- You have genuine context about their business
- Sequence timing aligns with their decision cycle

**Counterargument:**
- Breaks down if buyer is already actively shopping (comparing vendors)
- Requires domain expertise; fake specificity actually hurts
- Takes longer per lead than cold outreach (lower volume)

**Known Limits:**
- Works better in B2B than B2C
- Requires sales team to have domain depth
- Not scalable to 1M+ cold outreach campaigns

### 2. Product-Market Fit Diagnosis

**Statement:** PMF exists when NPS stays 50+ AND churn < 10% annually, maintained for 2+ quarters.

**Evidence:**
- Data from 5 different SaaS companies, 2015-2025
- Tested against failed products: high NPS + high churn (not PMF)
- Tested against succeeded products: both metrics together predicted growth

**How to Measure:**
- NPS 50+ means customers enthusiastically recommend (not just satisfied)
- Churn < 10% annually means you're not replacing customers
- Both together = retention is strong enough to fuel growth

**Failure Modes:**
- High churn + high NPS = product is great but wrong ICP (vibes ≠ fit)
- Low churn + low NPS = nobody's using it, stuck early cohort
- NPS decay over time = product fit is breaking (changing market or product)

**Counterargument:**
- NPS varies by industry (B2B vs SaaS vs marketplace)
- Churn measurement matters (MRR vs user-based)
- Can have PMF with lower numbers in enterprise

### 3. Team Scaling Culture Codification

**Statement:** Teams break predictably at transitions (3→5, 10→20, 30+ people). Breaks happen when founder's culture only exists in their head.

**Evidence:**
- Observed breaks at each transition in Dave's companies
- Founders who codified culture before scaling: smooth transitions
- Founders who scaled before codifying: cultural collapse after 15-20 people

**The Fix:**
- Document decision-making framework (how do we decide?)
- Codify values in hiring and conflict resolution
- Write the culture, don't assume it spreads by osmosis

**When To Do It:**
- Before scaling past 5 (when founder can't hire personally anymore)
- Before scaling past 20 (when founder can't know everyone)

**Known Limits:**
- Culture still requires founder reinforcement (can't document it away)
- Wrong people hired early are hard to course-correct
- Perfect culture doc won't save bad hiring
